01 / Research focus

The mechanics behind movement.

My work explores the relationship between movement strategies, mechanical loading and performance, with particular interests in lower-limb injury mechanisms and sport-specific tasks.

01

Injury mechanisms & joint stability

Investigating landing mechanics, fatigue and unanticipated movements, including ACL-related biomechanical factors and the proximal effects of chronic ankle instability.

ACLAnkle instabilityLanding
02

Modelling & movement measurement

Using musculoskeletal models to study internal mechanical demands, alongside evidence on the validity and reliability of wearable movement measurements.

AnyBodyIMUEvidence synthesis
03

Sport technique & performance

Examining sport-specific technique and performance in swimming, badminton and weightlifting, from individual movement phases to the demands of competition.

03 / About & approach

Connecting the lab to movement.

I am a doctoral student at Beijing Sport University. My research interests span sports biomechanics and rehabilitation, with a focus on ACL injury mechanisms, chronic ankle instability and athletic performance.

My publications combine experimental movement analysis, computational musculoskeletal modelling and systematic reviews to examine how movement and loading change across tasks and conditions.
