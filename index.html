Profile

I am a doctoral student at Beijing Sport University, working in sports biomechanics and rehabilitation. My research focuses on lower-limb injury mechanisms, joint stability and athletic performance.

I combine experimental movement analysis, musculoskeletal simulation and evidence synthesis to investigate how the body responds to sport-specific demands. My publications cover badminton, swimming, weightlifting and jumping, alongside collaborative work in public health and psychology.

I welcome collaboration in biomechanics, rehabilitation medicine and sports performance.

Research interests

01Injury biomechanics & joint stability

ACL injury mechanisms, chronic ankle instability, and the effects of fatigue and unanticipated tasks on lower-limb landing mechanics.

02Movement measurement & musculoskeletal modelling

Motion capture, force measurements, AnyBody simulation and wearable-sensor validation, supported by statistical parametric mapping and systematic evidence synthesis.

03Sports technique & performance

Biomechanical analysis of swimming, badminton, weightlifting and jumping, including race phases, movement coordination and performance-related interventions.
